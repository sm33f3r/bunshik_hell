## 1. Rules

| # | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One world is one episode.** Episodes release world by world and continue until NHMH decides to finish. | ✅ |
| 2 | **No more than 3 maps per episode.** | ✅ |
| 3 | **Every map has a map boss**: a recognizable food (or non-food) monster. | ✅ |
| 4 | **Every episode has one world final boss**: gigantic, absurd and difficult. An original monster that grows out of food and food culture but is unrecognizable as any one food. | ✅ |
| 5 | **Realism serves the satire.** Food, cuisine and settings are true to life. | ✅ |
| 6 | **Satire targets usually go unnamed.** Players recognize them from context. Parody names are decided later, if needed at all. ⚖️ Any satirical name for a real business needs a defamation and trademark check before release. | ✅ |
| 7 | **The 떡 has no mission.** He moves onward and survives. | ✅ |
| 8 | **Each episode has its own exit.** The way the 떡 leaves one episode (a toilet, vomited onto a street, thrown in a food-waste bin…) decides where the next begins. | ✅ concept / ❓ specifics |
| 9 | **The date is a mystery** and doesn't follow logical rules, so crossover characters can be any age. | ✅ |
| 10 | **Crossover characters** from other NHMH games appear alongside original characters. Who appears where is decided in session. | ✅ |
| 11 | **Episode names are placeholders** until each episode is named. | ✅ |
| 12 | Story is developed one step at a time, much of it after building begins. | ✅ |

## 3. Episodes

### Episode 1 — Yummy! Bunshik for Lunch (working title)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Theme | Daytime 분식 that school kids love. Lunchtime crowd. Almost no cigarette butts or soju bottle caps. ✅ |
| Maps | 1. **Lunch 분식집** resembling 김밥천국, with realistic menu choices ✅ · 2. School-gate street and 문방구 🟡 · 3. School cafeteria (급식실) ✅ map / 🟡 slot |
| Opening ✅ | The 떡 is speared on a **wooden pick** from a cup of 떡볶이. He slides off just before a woman puts him in her mouth. She is the first enemy or obstacle, stabbing at him again and again for a while. Probably in an indoor 분식집. |
| Map bosses | **Mutant 라볶이** ✅ (which map ❓) · others ❓ |
| World final boss | ❓ |
| Ending ✅ | After the final boss, the pick stabs the 떡 and the woman swallows him. Cliffhanger into Episode 2. |
| The woman | ❓ Identity undecided. |
| Realism note | Korean schools serve 급식, so kids mostly eat 분식 after school; the lunch crowd at a 김밥천국-style shop is mostly adults. 🔍 |
