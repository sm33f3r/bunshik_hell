# BUNSHIK HELL · 분식헬

You are one piece of 떡. No arms. No legs. No plan. Just sauce, dripping off you.

A woman spears you on a wooden pick and lifts you toward her mouth. You slide off.
She keeps stabbing. Welcome to Bunshik Hell.

Bunshik Hell is a grotesque, stupid, gorgeous 3D side-scrolling platformer set in a
nightmare version of Korean 분식 culture: the cheap lunch joints, the snack stands
outside the school gate, the carts, the counters, the pans of red sauce you crawled
out of. Everything here is food, and everything here wants you dead.

Every enemy plays by its own food logic. 만두 squash. 어묵 topple. 라면 drinks your
sauce and keeps coming. Some things you fight. Some things you run from screaming.
You won't always know which until it's too late.

You don't walk. You squiggle. You don't have a mission. You just keep going, because
the alternative is being eaten.

- One limbless rice cake against an entire food chain
- Squiggle, bounce, stomp, roll and slam; earn the sauce attacks you'll badly need
- Bosses that start as lunch and end as something nobody should have to look at
- Released in episodes, one world at a time, until we're done or dead
- Play on-chain with DDEOK rewards on Sui (after Episode 1), or off-chain. Your call
- Made by 니가 한국어 못해 (NHMH). The only NHMH game that teaches you absolutely nothing

**Status:** pre-alpha. Episode 1, *Yummy! Bunshik for Lunch*, is being built. Nothing
is playable yet.

**Engine:** Godot 4.7 (Forward+). Windows and Linux first.
